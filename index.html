    Go / no-go thresholds

    These match the starting values on the Weather Go/No-Go wiki page. Change them here to match what the team agrees on; this browser remembers your settings.

    The go / no-go verdict uses weather forecasts from Open-Meteo. The astronomy forecast is the Clear Sky Chart by Clear Dark Sky, from the Canadian Meteorological Centre's astronomy model. Thunderstorms always mean no-go: if you hear thunder, pack up and go indoors.
